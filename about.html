What I actually do

I've always liked making sense of data that looks messy at first glance — tracing a discrepancy back to its source, or turning a tangle of numbers into something clear. There's a real satisfaction in seeing a problem through to resolution, and that's what keeps this work fulfilling for me.

Currently working as a Data Analyst (independent contractor) for Crinkle Cut Digital, maintaining operational data records and coordinating data workflows across platforms for an AU-based client. Before that, six years managing purchase orders end-to-end at Vistaland and Lifescapes — most recently leading a team of four and standardising workflows that improved PO data accuracy by 30%.

I'm also comfortable working with AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini day-to-day — using them to speed up reporting, clean up messy data, and work through problems faster.
